About Janique

Janique Care Support was created to help Louisiana families feel calmer, more connected, and more supported through caring non-medical check-ins and communication.

Our story

Care from the heart, with family-first values.

Janique brings a compassionate caregiver mindset to every visit, call, and check-in. Her focus is simple: notice the small things early, share helpful updates, and make loved ones feel seen and supported.

This is a non-medical support service designed for families, seniors, disabled adults, hospice families, and people recovering at home who need extra reassurance and communication.

Why families trust this support

The goal is to reduce worry, improve communication, and help families stay informed without making medical claims.

Communication that helps

Families receive clear updates and a reliable point of contact for care-related check-ins.

Compassionate presence

Kindness, patience, and a calm approach are central to every part of the service.

Support at home

Designed for families who want extra peace of mind between visits, calls, and appointments.

Mission

To help families reduce worry and catch care concerns early through compassionate support, routine check-ins, and trusted communication.

Values

Respect, reliability, warmth, clarity, and family-centered care guide every interaction.
